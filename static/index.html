🎤 Dan's Comedy Clip Studio

Turn a full set into short vertical clips — from your phone.

1. Upload your set

Choose a video from your phone. MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM or M4V.

Ready when you are.

2. Review suggested moments

Tick the moments you want. Adjust start/end times in seconds. Suggestions are automated guesses — watch them before posting.

3. Download your clips

Your video is uploaded to the server for processing. Long videos can take a while. Keep this page open while transcription runs. The first transcription may download the selected speech model.
